# IFEC - Introdução à Física Estatística Computacional

**Course:** Introduction to Computational Statistical Physics  
**Semester:** 2024/2  
**University:** Universidade Federal de Minas Gerais (UFMG), Department of Computer Science (DCC)

Simulation of physical and complex systems. Each folder has the notebook, its PDF export and, in some cases, the statement and a standalone .py script.
